YM2203

The YM2203 is a sound chip introduced by Yamaha in the mid-1980's.
It is capable of playing both FM and PSG tones, which would later be used in many other Yamaha sound chips.
It was used in some arcade machines, as well as some Japan-exclusive personal computers such as the PC-88.

Features

Channels 3 FM, 3 PSG
Output Mono
Type of FM 4 operators
Codename OPN
