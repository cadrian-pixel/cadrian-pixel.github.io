YM2612

The YM2612 is an FM sound chip introduced by Yamaha around 1988.
It is most notable for being used in the Sega Genesis video game console, which is known as the Mega Drive outside of North America.

Features

Channels 6 FM, 1 PCM
Output Mono or Stereo
Type of FM 4 operators
Codename OPN2
