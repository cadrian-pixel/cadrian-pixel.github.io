YMZ280B

The YMZ280B is a sound chip introduced by Yamaha in the 1990's.
It can play back up to 8 channels of sampled recordings in 8-bit PCM, 16-bit PCM, or 4-bit ADPCM formats.
It was mostly used in arcade machines between the mid-1990's and the early 2000's.

Features

Channels 8 (PCM)
Output Mono or Stereo
Sample Formats 4-bit ADPCM, 8-bit PCM, 16-bit PCM
Sampling Rate 44.1 kHz (ADPCM), 88.2 kHz (PCM)
Codename PCMD8
